## Design Philosophy

CactusRalph-Coder is built around three core tenets:

- **Autonomy** — Ralph operates with minimal human intervention, self-correcting through feedback loops.
- **Measurability** — Every action Ralph takes is scored by the Ralph Meter, creating a continuous improvement signal.
- **Self-Evolution** — Ralph can rewrite, extend, and refine its own codebase through the Self-Building Codes engine.
